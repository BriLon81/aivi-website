What your own study shows you

Clarity on where you stand, and why

We work out the questions your customers actually ask, then put them to every major AI platform, over and over. You come away with a clear picture of four things.

01

Where you're named

How often each AI and Google names you: question by question, platform by platform, market by market.

02

Where competitors beat you

The questions, places and platforms where a competitor is named and you're not.

03

What the AI is reading

The websites behind the answers: which list you, which list your competitors, and what they say.

04

What changes over time

Ask again later and see what moved, for you and for them.

What we don't do: sell fixes, rankings or placements. We measure independently, so the numbers mean something.

Why ask more than once?

  • Ask an AI the same question twice and the businesses it names often change.
  • A single screenshot only shows what happened once.
  • We ask hundreds or thousands of times, across platforms and days.

So what we report is a rate, not a guess.
