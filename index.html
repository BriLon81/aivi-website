The shift

How patients find a dentist is changing — and it's measurable.

The way people choose a local provider has moved through three eras. We don't ask you to take the shift on faith. We measure where it stands today.

Yesterday

Patients Googled and clicked

Practices surfaced on keywords, backlinks, and page speed. SEO agencies owned that playbook — and a whole industry grew up to game it.

Traditional SEO
Today

Patients ask AI and trust the answer

ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini answer directly — often with no click at all. A few practices get named; the rest are simply absent from the conversation.

AI answers directly
Tomorrow

Agents book on a patient's behalf

As assistants start scheduling appointments directly, a practice AI doesn't name with confidence won't even make the shortlist.

Agentic booking
We don't predict this — we measure it. Yesterday and Tomorrow are context. Today is a fact: we ran the queries, recorded every practice named, and counted. What you get from us is the measurement, not a forecast.
