The audit

12 signals AI search actually uses

These are the factors that determine whether AI recommends your practice — or your competitor's.

🏷️

Practice name clarity

Machine-readable brand signals so AI can confidently name and cite your practice.

📍

NAP consistency

Name, address, and phone number consistent across every page and directory listing.

🗂️

Schema markup

Structured data that tells AI exactly what type of practice you are and where you're located.

👤

Provider credentials

Practitioner bios with full credentials — AI skips businesses that appear unlicensed.

❓

FAQ quality & coverage

Machine-readable FAQ content gives AI something to cite when answering patient questions.

⭐

Review signals

Review volume and recency — AI deprioritizes unvalidated businesses in competitive markets.

🔍

Indexability

Ensuring your core pages are actually crawlable and accessible to AI and search systems.

🚨

Emergency & urgent intent

Coverage for urgent appointment queries — high-conversion traffic most practices miss.

💳

Insurance & payment clarity

Clear payment information removes friction and improves AI recommendation confidence.

🩺

Service coverage

Specific services clearly described — required for AI to match your offering to patient searches.

📞

Contact & location page

A clear, dedicated location page is fundamental for local AI discovery.

🏆

Title & location signals

Homepage titles that include your specialty and location — essential for geographic relevance.
