How it works

  1. Step 1

    Tell us about you

    What you offer, where, and who you compete with.

  2. Step 2

    We set the questions

    From evidence of how your customers really ask. We test the wording first, and show you the questions before the main run.

  3. Step 3

    We ask, over and over

    Every question, on ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and Google, many times over several days.

  4. Step 4

    You get the picture

    A clear report, private to you, and a free check-up six weeks later. Keep tracking monthly or quarterly if it's useful.

What counts as a market? One kind of business in one place, named the way your customers name it when they ask. "HVAC in The Beaches" and "HVAC in Toronto" are different markets. In our Toronto dentist study, every neighbourhood got its own answer. We work out which ones matter to you when we set the questions.

What we don't do

We don't sell fixes, rankings or placements, and we never charge anyone to appear in a study. We measure independently, so the numbers mean something.

The measurement is ours. You get your full results, privately. Other businesses in your market can get their own results too, and we never tell them who asked for the study. Tracking subscribers can keep their market's results to themselves.
