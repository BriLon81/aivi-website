About

An independent measurement company for AI search.

AIVI Labs exists to answer one question with evidence: when people ask AI for a local business, who does it actually name? We measure it, and we report the facts — nothing more, nothing we made up.

Who we are

We're a small, independent team. We don't sell SEO, we don't take money to influence AI answers, and we don't grade anyone on a curve. Our only product is the measurement — how often each business is named across the AI surfaces real customers use — and the credibility of that number depends entirely on our staying independent of the businesses we measure.
