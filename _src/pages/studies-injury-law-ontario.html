Studies / Personal injury law

Personal injury law across Ontario

We asked AI and Google for the best injury lawyer in 20 Ontario cities, 2,400 times, and recorded which law firms were named.

The short answer

Each city gets its own answer. One law firm was named in more than half of the Toronto answers, but in fewer than 1 in 10 of the Ottawa answers.

2,400 asks · 20 cities · 6 platforms · Oct 2026

What this means for a law firm

  • Each city is its own market. One firm was named in 56% of Toronto answers and 7% of Ottawa’s. Finding 04 ↓
  • Outside Toronto, the firm named most was usually a local one. That was true in 16 of the 19 other cities. Finding 01 ↓
  • Being listed on the websites AI uses most isn’t enough to get named. All three firms we checked were listed in cities where AI rarely named them. Finding 05 ↓
  • ChatGPT rarely uses a firm’s own website. Claude, Gemini and Perplexity often do. Finding 06 ↓

If you market law firms, or run another professional practice that serves several cities, such as accounting or financial advice, the same questions apply to you.

What we found

Seven things we found

Measuring a whole province also shows how the AIs behave. These are the patterns we saw. No firm is named.

01

In most cities, the firm AI named most was a local one, not a Toronto firm.

There are 19 cities in the study besides Toronto. In 16 of them, the firm named most often was a local or regional firm. One more city had a tie for first.

05

Being listed on the websites AI uses most isn’t enough to get named.

Firms were usually named most in the cities where they were listed on the websites AI used most. But all three firms we checked were also listed on those websites in cities where AI rarely named them (12% of answers or fewer).

06

ChatGPT rarely uses a law firm’s own website. The other AIs often do.

Three firms named across Ontario had their own website used as a source in 20% to 39% of all AI answers. ChatGPT almost never used them: in only 14 to 20 of its 400 answers. For one of the firms, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity each used its website in more than half of their answers.

07

Ask Claude again a week later and its top firm can change. Count all six platforms and it doesn’t.

We asked Claude the same questions again nine days later, this time letting it give full-length answers. Its most-named firm changed in 4 of the 20 cities. But with all six platforms counted together, the most-named firm stayed the same in every city. One answer, or one AI, isn’t enough to go on.

What we asked

Two everyday questions, in each city:

  • best personal injury lawyer in [city], Ontario
  • best car accident lawyer in [city], Ontario

This study ran before we made our question-setting step standard. These are two common ways people search. We didn't first check how people actually ask or test other wordings, as we do now. Future rounds will.

How we asked

  • Six platforms: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Google's AI answer and Google's map results.
  • Ten times each: every question asked 10 times on every platform in every city, 15–17 Oct 2026. Claude was asked again on 24–26 Oct 2026 with full-length answers.
  • Twenty cities: Toronto, Ottawa, Mississauga, Brampton, Hamilton, London, Markham, Vaughan, Kitchener, Windsor, Oakville, Burlington, Oshawa, Barrie, St. Catharines, Guelph, Kingston, Sudbury, Thunder Bay and Peterborough.
  • Every answer kept: recorded in full, with the firms it named and the websites it used as sources.

A note on our numbers

These are patterns we observed, not causes. For "named in 12 or more cities", a firm counts in a city when it appears in at least 3 answers there. Each question was asked 10 times per platform in each city, so city-level figures are a first read, not a final word. The AI answers and the map results were collected from a Canada-wide location. A firm counts when its own name appears in an answer; answers that name only a lawyer aren't counted for the firm, and different names for the same firm are counted together. Google's AI answer is counted only when Google showed one (269 of 400 searches).
