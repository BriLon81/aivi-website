What we asked

Four everyday questions, phrased the way people type them:

  • best dentist in [city]
  • top dentists in [city]
  • best family dentist in [city]
  • who is the best dentist in [city]

How we asked

  • Six platforms: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Google's AI answer and Google's map results.
  • Over and over: each question repeated many times, across different days and times of day.
  • Two lists: 31 dental businesses in Peterborough and 26 in Belleville.
  • Every answer kept: recorded in full, with the practices it named matched against the list.

Along the way

Seven things we noticed about how AI answers

Measuring a city this thoroughly also shows how the platforms behave. These are the patterns we saw, not the rankings.

01

How you phrase the question decides whether Google writes an AI answer.

Typed as keywords ("best dentist in Belleville"), Google showed an AI answer only 1–3% of the time. Asked as a question ("who is the best dentist in Belleville?"), it showed one 84–86% of the time. Capitals, punctuation and "who's" versus "who is" made no difference. Only whether it was a question.

02

Google's AI answer and Google's map don't agree.

Of the five practices each one named most, the AI answer and the map shared only three in Peterborough and two in Belleville. One Peterborough practice was in 2 of every 3 Google AI answers, and never once on Google's map.

03

Some practices are never named at all.

In Peterborough, 4 of the 31 dental businesses on our list were not named once, on any platform, in more than 5,000 answers.

04

Being named goes with being read.

In both cities, no practice was named often without the AI also reading its website. We can't say which comes first: AI may name what it can read, or read what it already names.

05

A handful of websites stand between a town's dentists and the AI.

When the AIs cited a source other than a practice's own site, five or six review and directory websites made up most of it. And each AI has a favourite: ChatGPT leaned on one review site, Claude on others, while Perplexity and Google's AI answer cited practices' own websites most.

06

Some AIs name dentists. Others name clinics.

Claude and Gemini named an individual dentist ("Dr. …") in almost every answer. ChatGPT and Perplexity did so in roughly 60–75% of answers, and more often named the clinic.

07

Ask for "the best" and some AIs won't choose.

Asked "who is the best dentist in [city]?", Claude said some version of "there's no single best" in more than 9 answers out of 10. Asked for "top dentists", it almost never did, and simply gave a list.
