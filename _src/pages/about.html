About AIVI Labs

AIVI Labs measures what AI assistants say about businesses. We ask the questions customers really ask, many times over, on ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and Google, and report how often each business is named, and what the AI was reading when it answered.

Why we do it

More people ask AI first. Few businesses know what it says.

People now ask an AI who to call, where to go and what to buy. The answers change from one ask to the next, differ from one AI to another, and shift when the models are updated. One screenshot can't tell a business where it stands. We built the tools to measure it properly, and we publish what we find along the way.

Independent by design

We measure. We don't sell the fix.

No fixes, rankings or placements

We don't sell ways to be named more, so we have no reason to tell you anything but what we measured.

No one pays to appear

We never charge a business to be included in a study.

Our questions are public

Every question we asked is published with the study, so you can judge them yourself.

We say how accurate we are

Every report states how accurate its numbers are, and we only sell work measured to one standard.
