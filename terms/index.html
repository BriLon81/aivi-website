05 What our results are, and what they aren't

Our results record what AI platforms and Google said when we asked them our questions, during the period we measured.

  • They describe a moment. AI answers change from one ask to the next, and when platforms update their models. We measure many times so the numbers mean something, and every report says how accurate its numbers are. A later measurement can still differ.
  • They are a measurement. They aren't a ranking, an endorsement, a certification or a prediction, and nothing we provide guarantees that a business will be named more often.
  • We report what the AI said, not whether it's true. AI answers can contain mistakes about a business, such as an old address or a closed office. We record them as given.
  • We don't control the AI platforms or Google, and we aren't affiliated with them. Their names are trademarks of their owners.
  • We don't sell fixes, and we never charge a business to be included in a study.
