Where AI gets its answers

When AI names a dentist or a law firm, it's usually repeating something it found on another website. We recorded more than 70,000 of the sources AI used to build its answers.

What we found

Seven things we noticed about what AI reads

From our studies of dentists and injury lawyers. No business or website is named.

01

Each AI uses its own set of websites.

We asked each AI the same questions about the same city, and each one used different websites to answer. For Belleville dentists, ChatGPT used one review website as a source 838 times. Claude never used it. For Toronto injury lawyers, ChatGPT relied most on a lawyer-ranking guide, while Claude relied on two "best of" pages that the other AIs barely used.

A few websites supply most of what AI reads.

Leave out the dentists’ own websites, and just five or six organisations supplied most of the sources the AIs used: 74% in Peterborough and 64% in Belleville.

03

AI named a dentist often only when it had also read the dentist’s own website.

In both dental cities, no practice was named often unless the AI had also used its own website as a source. In Toronto, the website of one of the most-named law firms was used by two AIs and never by the other three. We can’t tell which comes first: AI may name the businesses whose websites it reads, or read the websites of businesses it already names.

04

Much of what AI reads is "best of" lists.

For Toronto injury lawyers, many of the pages AI used most were "top 10" style articles on general websites or websites from outside Toronto, and most didn’t say whether they were sponsored. One was a list of Toronto’s top injury lawyers published by a news site in Florida. Lawyer rankings and directories were used too, but they were only part of the picture.

05

A brand-new page can quickly become the one AI uses most.

In our Toronto injury law study, the page the AIs used most as a source had gone online just ten days before we asked. Four different AIs were already using it.

06

AI can read that a firm is "#1" and still never name it.

One article called a law firm "the #1 choice". One AI used that article as a source in all 50 of its answers about Toronto injury lawyers, and named that firm in none of them. Using a page doesn’t mean repeating what it says.

07

Some of the pages AI reads say bad things about businesses.

One page, which a single AI used in 47 of its 50 answers about Toronto injury lawyers, included complaints about fees and slow responses at four of the city’s most-named firms. Most businesses don’t know which of these pages exist.

A note on our numbers

These are patterns we observed, not causes. We record which websites the AIs use and what they say; we don't claim that one causes the other. The dental figures come from two cities measured over the summer; the legal figures come from a smaller Toronto study (50 answers per platform), so treat those as early signals.
