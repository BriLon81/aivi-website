Studies / Toronto

Toronto, neighbourhood by neighbourhood

We asked ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's map for dentists in 186 areas of Toronto, more than 14,000 times.

The short answer

To AI, every Toronto neighbourhood is its own market. Ask for a dentist in Bloor West Village, then in Etobicoke next door, and not one dentist appears on both lists.

14,000+ searches · 186 areas · 3 platforms · Aug–Oct 2026

ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's map results. All 158 of the City of Toronto's official neighbourhoods, plus the names locals actually use (the Annex, King West, the Danforth, Liberty Village and others).

What this means for a business in a big city

  • Every neighbourhood gets its own answer. On most platforms, all 158 official neighbourhoods got their own list. Finding 01 ↓
  • Next door can be a different market. Bloor West Village and Etobicoke had no dentists in common. Finding 02 ↓
  • The name people use matters. “Little Italy” and “Palmerston-Little Italy” got noticeably different lists. Finding 04 ↓
  • The answers move. Six weeks later, about half the dentists ChatGPT and Gemini named had changed. Finding 06 ↓

If you run several locations, are choosing where to open, or market businesses across a city, the same questions apply to you.

What we found

Seven things we found

No practice is named.

01

Every neighbourhood gets its own answer.

We expected Toronto’s 158 official neighbourhoods to group into a few dozen bigger markets. They didn’t. On most platforms, every one of them got its own answer. The only areas that merged were two names for the same place, like “the Annex” and “Annex”.

02

Next door can get a completely different list.

Bloor West Village and Etobicoke sit side by side, and not one dentist was named for both, on any platform. King West and Liberty Village are a short walk apart and shared almost none. Even downtown, the Fashion District, the Entertainment District and “Downtown Toronto” each got their own list.

03

AI names about the same number of dentists, however many there are.

A neighbourhood with two dentists inside its borders still gets about a dozen dentists named, drawn from the streets around it. A neighbourhood with fifty gets about the same.

04

What you call the place changes the answer.

Simple names for the same place got the same dentists: “the Junction” and “Junction Area”, “Roncey” and “Roncesvalles”. But the city’s longer official names didn’t match the names people use. “Palmerston-Little Italy” and “Little Italy” got noticeably different lists, and so did “Kensington-Chinatown” and “Kensington”.

05

Sometimes Google’s map showed nothing. The AIs always named someone.

For three neighbourhoods, Google showed no map results in any of 25 tries. For two more, it mostly showed none. ChatGPT and Gemini named dentists for all 186 areas, every time.

06

Six weeks later, about half the dentists AI named had changed.

We asked about seven areas in August and again six weeks later. On ChatGPT and Gemini, roughly half the dentists named had been replaced. Google’s map changed less, about a quarter. Within each round the answers were steady; between rounds, they moved.

07

Chain clinics were named more often than independents.

We looked at the 79 clinics of Canada’s two largest dental groups: 66 were named at least once by ChatGPT or Gemini. Of the 1,177 independent practices with their own website, 730 were. We didn’t adjust for size, age, reviews or marketing spend, so this shows who was named, not why.

A note on our numbers

This study used ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's map results; Claude and Perplexity weren't part of it. The final analysis was completed on 26 October 2026. ChatGPT changed its answer format partway through the study; we recorded it and checked that it doesn't change the headline. The six-week comparison can't separate time from model changes, so we report how much the answers moved, not why. Finding 07 was recounted on 2 November 2026: "named" means named at least once by ChatGPT or Gemini, with no minimum number of asks. One group's clinics were partly identified from AI answers, which flatters its figure; the other group on its own comes out at 81% (38 of 47).
