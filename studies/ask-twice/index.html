Studies / Ask twice

Ask twice: how consistent are AI answers?

We asked identical questions thousands of times on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and Google, and compared the answers.

The short answer

One answer is a roll of the dice. Ask Gemini the same question twice and only about 2 in 5 of the businesses it names appear both times. But ask hundreds of times, and how often a business is named holds steady to within 2 to 4 points.

50,000+ answers · 6 platforms · May–Nov 2026

What this means for a business

  • One screenshot proves very little. Ask again and the list of businesses can change. Finding 02 ↓
  • How often you’re named can be measured. Over hundreds of asks, it holds steady to within 2 to 4 points. Finding 01 ↓
  • An AI update can move you overnight. After a Gemini update in July, one practice went from being named in 54% of answers to 84%. Finding 04 ↓
  • Some AIs drift from day to day. Perplexity’s answers changed more between days than within one; Gemini varied on every ask. Finding 06 ↓

This applies to any business people ask AI about, and to agencies that report AI results to their clients.

What we found

Six things we found

From repeated questions across our studies. No business is named.

01

Over hundreds of asks, how often a business is named holds steady.

Count how often a business is named across hundreds of asks, and on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Perplexity that figure is accurate to within 2 to 4 points. That’s what makes it a number you can trust, when a single answer isn’t.

02

But any single list changes a lot.

Ask Gemini the same question twice and, of all the businesses it names across the two answers, only about 35–40% appear in both. Claude is steadier, at around 55–60%. Google’s map is the steadiest, at around 80%.

03

Who gets named first depends on the platform.

On Google’s map, the same business comes first in about 8 of every 10 repeats. On Gemini, the first name changes far more often, and one question can produce half a dozen different “first” answers over a study.

04

An AI update nobody announced moved businesses by 30 points.

In July, Google switched the version of Gemini most people use. Same questions, same city, old version against new: one practice went from being named 54% of the time to 84%. Another fell from 75% to 46%. The normal wobble is a couple of points.

05

AI answers can change shape overnight.

On 8 October, in the middle of one of our studies, ChatGPT’s answers changed format from one day to the next: longer, with more bold text and more lists. Earlier version changes on the same questions halved answer lengths, or made an AI far more likely to say “it depends”.

Typical number of words in a ChatGPT answer, 7 October → 8 October

219 → 361

06

Some AIs drift from day to day. Others vary on every ask.

Perplexity’s answers on the same day look more alike than its answers on different days, so it drifts over time. Gemini varies just as much within an hour as across days. That’s why we spread every study across several days and times.
