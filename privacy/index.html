01 Overview

AIVI Labs SEZC ("AIVI Labs", "we", "us") builds and runs software that measures what AI assistants say about businesses. This policy explains what personal information we handle, why, who we share it with, and the rights you have.

It covers our website (aivilabs.io and our report pages), the forms on it, our studies, and how we contact businesses. AIVI Labs is the data controller. We're based in the Cayman Islands and follow the Cayman Islands Data Protection Act (2021 Revision).

We don't collect patient or client records from the businesses we study, and we never receive payment card details.

03 How we use it

  • To reply to study requests and run the studies you order.
  • To send findings emails to people who signed up.
  • To deliver practice reports and order emails.
  • To measure how often businesses are named by AI, and to publish findings about how AI answers. We don't publish which businesses were named. Each business can get its own results.
  • To contact businesses named in our studies about their results. This is business-to-business only, and every email lets you opt out.
  • To keep our service secure, for example by limiting how many form submissions come from one address.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

We don't sell personal information. We don't make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

04 Who we share it with

We use service providers that handle data on our behalf:

  • GitHub: hosts aivilabs.io and stores our private records.
  • Cloudflare: runs our domain names and hosts our report pages.
  • DigitalOcean: the servers that receive form submissions and run our measurements.
  • Postmark: sends our emails.
  • Google Workspace: our email and spreadsheets.
  • Make.com: automates the delivery of orders.
  • Dodo Payments: our Merchant of Record for purchases.
  • Google Fonts: serves the typefaces on our report pages.
  • AI platforms and DataForSEO: when we run a study, we send our questions to OpenAI, Google, Anthropic and Perplexity, and to DataForSEO for Google's results. These questions don't contain information about you.
  • Business data providers: we use them to find business contact details.

We also share information when the law requires it, or to protect our rights.

Some of these providers handle data outside the Cayman Islands, including in the United States and Canada. We only use providers that protect it appropriately.

05 Payments

Dodo Payments Inc. is the Merchant of Record and handles all payment details. We never see your card details. Dodo sends us a short confirmation (order ID, product and email address) so we can deliver your order. Dodo's privacy policy covers checkout.

06 Cookies, pixels and tracking

aivilabs.io has no tracking pixels or tags of any kind:

  • no Meta Pixel;
  • no Google Analytics or Google Ads tags;
  • no session recording;
  • no other advertising or analytics code.

AIVI Labs sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. We don't measure page views or where visitors come from. Nothing about your visit is shared with Meta, Google or any advertising network.

Our emails contain no tracking pixels either. We don't track whether you open them or click their links.

What does leave your browser:

  • Pages: GitHub, which hosts aivilabs.io, receives your IP address when it serves a page. Our typefaces are served from our own site.
  • Report pages: our dental report pages (on get.aivilabs.io and reports.aivilabs.io) are served by Cloudflare, and they load their typefaces from Google Fonts. So Cloudflare and Google receive your IP address and browser details when you open one. Cloudflare may also set a strictly necessary security cookie.
  • Forms: nothing is sent until you press the button. Our server records your IP address with each submission, for security and to limit abuse.

Checkout happens on Dodo Payments' site, which has its own cookie and tracking practices.

07 How long we keep it

  • Study requests and client records: while we work together, then 5 years for accounting and legal reasons.
  • Order records: at least 5 years.
  • Findings emails: until you unsubscribe. We keep a record of your consent and of your unsubscribe, so we don't email you again.
  • Business contact details used for outreach: until you ask us to stop. After that, we keep only what's needed to keep you off our list.
  • Study data: kept indefinitely as research records, so that published findings can be checked.
  • Website checks for dental reports: up to 24 months.
  • Server logs with IP addresses: a limited period, for security.

08 Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct anything that's wrong;
  • delete it, unless we have to keep it by law;
  • stop using it, including for outreach or in our study data;
  • stop sending you marketing emails (every email has an unsubscribe link);
  • send it to you in a portable format, where that applies.

You can also withdraw your consent at any time. Email support@aivilabs.io. We'll reply within 30 days.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain:

  • Cayman Islands: to the Office of the Ombudsman (ombudsman.ky).
  • Elsewhere: if you're in the EEA, the UK or Canada, you may also have rights under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or PIPEDA.

We rely on these grounds:

  • Contract: to deliver what you ordered.
  • Legitimate interests: to run our studies, contact businesses about their results, and keep the service secure.
  • Consent: for findings emails.

09 Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), limit access to the people who need it, and use established service providers. No method of sending or storing data is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

10 Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you think a minor has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

11 Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the effective date at the top. For significant changes, we may also email our customers.
