01 Overview

AIVI Labs sells practice reports as one-time digital purchases, and studies and tracking subscriptions by written quote. Online purchases are processed by Dodo Payments Inc, which acts as the Merchant of Record for those transactions.

We want every customer to be satisfied with their purchase. If you are not happy for any reason, you may request a refund in accordance with this policy.

02 Refund Window

You may request a refund within 14 days of your purchase date. Refund requests made within this 14-day window will be honored and processed by Dodo Payments.

To be eligible for a refund, simply contact us within 14 days of your purchase using the process described below.

Studies and tracking ordered by quote follow the refund terms in the quote.

03 How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email us at support@aivilabs.io with:

  • Your order ID (from your confirmation email)
  • The email address used at checkout
  • A brief description of the reason for your request

We will acknowledge your request within 1 business day and aim to resolve it within 5 business days. Approved refunds are processed by Dodo Payments and typically return to your original payment method within 5–10 business days, depending on your card issuer.

04 Cancelling a Tracking Subscription

You can cancel a tracking subscription at any time by emailing support@aivilabs.io before your next monthly or quarterly period begins. Cancelling stops future measurement rounds. Rounds already delivered are not refunded. We confirm every cancellation by email within 1 business day.

05 EU & UK Consumer Rights

If you are located in the European Union or United Kingdom, you have a statutory right to cancel a digital service contract within 14 days of purchase under the Consumer Rights Directive and related regulations.

Dodo Payments, as the Merchant of Record, is responsible for handling refund requests that fall under EU and UK statutory consumer rights. If you are an EU or UK customer and wish to exercise your statutory rights, you may also contact Dodo Payments directly through their support portal.

06 Dodo Payments as Merchant of Record

All AIVI Labs online purchases are transacted through Dodo Payments Inc, which acts as the Merchant of Record. Dodo Payments is responsible for payment processing, tax collection, and is the entity that appears on your bank or card statement.

For billing disputes or payment issues, you may contact Dodo Payments directly through their support portal. For product-related issues, contact AIVI Labs directly at support@aivilabs.io.
