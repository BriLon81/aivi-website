Methodology

How we measure — and what the number means.

Everything we report is a measured fact, not a score we invented. Here's exactly how each mention rate is produced, and where we draw the line on precision.

01 · What we ask

We ask AI what a real patient would ask.

For each city we run 1,000+ measurement queries every quarter, phrased the way a prospective patient actually asks. We record every practice named in every response, then aggregate.

Run counts vary by design from quarter to quarter — we sample more heavily when we are testing a platform change — but never fall below the 1,000+ floor, and the query set stays the same. That is what makes the rates comparable across quarters. The exact count for any given quarter is published with that quarter's figures.

03 · How we count

Mention rate, with an honest denominator.

Your mention rate is simple: of all the answers we collected on a surface, what share named your practice. The denominator is the total number of runs on that surface — not just the answers you happened to appear in. Runs where no local practice was named still count, because being absent from those answers is exactly the thing we're measuring.

Google AI Overviews is measured only on searches where an AI Overview actually appeared — the honest denominator for that surface.

04 · How precise we claim to be

Integer rates, and a stated noise floor.

Repeated measurements across the panel establish a noise floor of about ±6 percentage points at the aggregate level. Because the data doesn't support more precision than that, we report whole-number rates only — no false decimals, no fabricated exactness.

This is deliberate. A number that looks more precise than the measurement behind it is a score dressed up as a fact. We'd rather give you a rate you can trust than a decimal you can't.

05 · Why it doesn't stay still

AI answers shift, so we remeasure.

AI systems change their answers over time as models and indexes update. A measurement is a snapshot of a moment, and we say so. Reports carry the date they were measured and a suggested remeasurement window, so you're never reading a stale number as if it were today's.

What AIVI Labs is

AIVI Labs is the measurement layer. We don't sell SEO services. We don't make recommendations to AI platforms. We measure what's happening — how often each practice is named — and we report it. That independence is the whole point.
